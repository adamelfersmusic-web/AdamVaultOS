Escensus · Training Mode

Your reps, saved — and sent to your trainer.

Your trainer assigns the reps. You knock them out on your phone. They can see you did them, and hear every take. This is how you get coached without being on a call together.

Before you start — what this does

  • ✓Your practice reps are recorded and saved.
  • ✓They're delivered to your trainer, who can listen back and see your progress.
  • ✓You can play your own reps back any time — hear yourself improve.
Recording & sharing is on only while Training Mode is active, and only for the reps you record here. Improving the system with anonymized data is a separate choice you make later — never bundled with this.

Prototype. In this demo your reps are saved on this device and the trainer delivery is simulated — the real version syncs to your trainer's dashboard. Every line here is Escensus's own phrasing.
