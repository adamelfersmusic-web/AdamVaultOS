05
What compounds

Month one is a better report. Month twelve is memory.

  1. Month 1 Some scored calls. Modestly useful. Honestly — a slightly better report.
  2. Month 3 Per-agent trajectories become visible. “Mike's price beat went from 40% to 70%.” Coaching now has evidence behind it.
  3. Month 6 Decision history exists. “What changed after the rebuttal drill?” has a real answer, with a date attached.
  4. Month 12 Institutional memory that outlives personnel. A new hire's ramp compared against the historical curve. Questions you couldn't have formulated in month one.
