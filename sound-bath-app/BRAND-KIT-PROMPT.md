# Brand + marketing kit — handoff

For a **fresh session** on the repo. Not the session that built the app — that
one read `VISION.md` §F at hour one, so its palette and type opinions are
already in context and cannot be scoped out after the fact.

The whole design of this prompt: **one file in, one direction out.** The visual
system comes only from the app; the words are inlined so no other file needs
opening.

---

```
I need a brand kit and marketing kit for Bed — a tool for sound bath
practitioners who play crystal singing bowls and lead group sessions for rooms
of 40–100 people lying on the floor in candlelight.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
READ EXACTLY ONE FILE

  sound-bath-app/app/index.html

That is the working app. Read it, run it, look at every screen — library,
perform, follower, shape, ensemble, the wizard. The visual language in it was
built with real care, and those decisions ARE the brand. Your job is to make
them explicit, consistent and extendable. Not to invent a new direction, and
not to second-guess the one that's there.

DO NOT OPEN: VISION.md, BUILD-BRIEF-V1.md, BOOK.html, BOOK.pdf,
BRAND-BRIEF.md, FABLE-PROMPT.md, or anything under prototype/.

Those hold palette, type and reference opinions written before the app existed,
plus screenshots of earlier unrelated prototypes. They would pull you toward a
direction that isn't the app's. Everything you need in words is below.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
THE WORDS — this is all of them, use them as the source of truth

THE PROMISE
  You already have the love, the instruments, and the presence.
  This is the first thing that lets you lead with them.

Not "make better sound baths." The buyer loves sound, has collected bowls for
years, and can hold a room — but cannot compose, arrange or run a DAW, and that
gap is the only thing between them and leading a real session. They spend $800
on a single bowl without blinking. They are not looking for cheap and not
looking for clinical.

THE POSTURE
The hero is always the person, never the software. The product's job is to
disappear. It must never look like it is doing the healing — the humans are.

THE LINE
"One violin player becomes a symphony."

THE STORY
Thursday. Musicians you love are passing through town. There's no event
planned. By Saturday there are sixty people on the floor, because you spent
fifteen minutes building a structure and the rest of the evening being present.
The promise isn't better sound baths — it's that you can say yes on Thursday.

THE FILM — two minutes about a Thursday that turned into something, with only
three cuts of a screen in the entire piece. That ratio is the philosophy: the
technical layer disappears.
  1. Thursday. A text lands — musicians you love are passing through.
  2. A beautiful room with plants. Tea being poured. Tarot on a low table.
  3. Two friends over a laptop. Fifteen minutes of structure.      [screen 1]
  4. Phones syncing — the pulse.                                    [screen 2]
  5. Bowls out of a canvas bag. Candles lit. Mats laid down.
  6. People arriving who didn't know this existed on Monday.
  7. Eighty seconds of the room. Live players. Light. Sixty people on the floor.
  8. A hand raised mid-session, and the room changes. No caption.   [screen 3]
  9. After. Someone stacking mats, smiling. "We made it happen."
Shot 8 is the insider shot — outsiders read atmosphere, practitioners sit up,
because they know how hard that is with ten people and no click track. Keep the
pronoun in the closing line: "we" is the people in the room, not the app.

NEVER SAY
- "Your audience can't tell the difference." True, and it reads as contempt for
  the craft to exactly the people whose endorsement matters most.
- "AI." In this market the word signals inauthentic, extractive, machine-made
  spirituality.
- Anything about what a frequency does to a body. Labels only. No health claims.

DO SAY
The sound design was never the art — leading the room is. This removes what was
blocking you from what you're actually good at.

If you find a genuinely better line while working, propose it in a note rather
than silently replacing these. They're settled, not sacred.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
DELIVERABLES

BRAND KIT — extracted from the app, not invented
1. Wordmark as it stands, plus a compact form that reads at 20px and as a
   favicon.
2. The palette with hex values, and what each colour MEANS. Exactly one colour
   means "this is live and making sound," and nothing else may ever use it —
   state that rule explicitly and make sure every artefact you produce honours
   it, including the landing page.
3. The type pairing, with the performance sizes the app already uses. Say why
   those sizes — they were chosen for four feet, not a desk.
4. The section colour arc — which hues, which order, and why.
5. Motion rules: what moves, what doesn't, why.
6. Where you made a judgment call the app didn't settle, say so and say why.
   Those are the most interesting parts of any brand book.

MARKETING KIT
7. A landing page. Dark. It has to survive the same room the app does — a page
   that only works on white contradicts the product.
8. The film treatment as a shot list you'd actually hand a director.
9. Three to five lines of copy for a page or a card.
10. The one screenshot you'd lead with, and why that one.
11. A short rationale — the position you took, and specifically what you
    rejected. What you turned down says more than what you kept.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
THE ROOM — the constraint behind every judgment

Dark, washed in warm amber from candlelight and tungsten. Screens read at about
four feet, at an oblique angle. The person reading has a mallet in each hand.
Warm colours vanish into that wash; cool colours survive it. A bright or white
screen there is genuinely disrespectful and everyone present knows it.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
ONE APP CHANGE WHILE YOU'RE IN THERE

Add a band status row to the performance screen: SUB / DRONE / MOTIF / TEXTURE,
filled when sounding, hollow when silent. It surfaces the four-band
architecture as live information a leader can act on mid-session.
```
