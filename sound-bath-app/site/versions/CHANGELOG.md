## v2 — the entry line and the small room · 2026-07-31

**Nothing was removed. Two things were added**, both between the Thursday story
and *How it holds*.

### 1 · The entry line

> ### Two bowls and Bed is a sound bath.
> ### Seven bowls and Bed is an orchestra.

Ratified by Adam the day it was written. Every other line on the page speaks to
someone who **already leads**. This is the only one that answers the first
question of someone who **doesn't yet** — *what do I need to start?* A seven-bowl
set is $1,500–5,000 and it is the wall that stops most people **before** their
first attempt rather than after it.

It scales with whoever reads it: two bowls and you're already in, seven and
you're being told you're more than you thought. **Nobody lands outside it.**

Under it, one paragraph stating the mechanism plainly — the sheet is worked out
from the bowls *you* own — because a claim that good is only worth making if
it's obviously not a trick.

### 2 · The small room

> *A Tuesday evening, twelve people, a studio with the lights off. Two bowls in
> your hands and forty minutes that go somewhere…*

**A counterweight, and it fixes a contradiction v1 had without knowing it.**
Every scene on this page is sixty people in candlelight — the aspiration, and it
stays exactly as big as it is. But once the page says *two bowls is enough*, a
reader with two bowls and a Tuesday class would still read themselves out of the
pictures while the sentence above invited them in.

**Deliberately not a persona.** No yoga teacher is named anywhere. Naming a
segment reads the other segment out, and it hands the named one a smaller
identity than the one they're reaching toward — and *the hero is always the
person*. The scene does the work that naming would have done, without the cost.

### What did not change
The masthead, the promise, the hero, the device frame, the Thursday story, the
*How it holds* columns, the five laws, the CTAs, and every amber decision. **v1
is intact inside v2** — read the diff and it's two insertions.
